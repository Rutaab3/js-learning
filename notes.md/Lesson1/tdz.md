> <h1> <center>temporal dead zone(tdz)


_period between the start of scope and declaration of a variable (__only in let and const__)._


```js
console.log(a);       //start of scope
//temporal dead zone
//temporal dead zone
//temporal dead zone
let a = 10;           //declaration of variable
```
